Invasive species are often portrayed as outside of the ecosystem, as something that unilaterally harms the ecosystem. Dan Egan tries to approach discussing invasive species as a story of history, both human and natural, and evaluate the impacts of invasive species in the great lakes through time and how the role of invasive species changes through time. 
<!--more-->
The example I like the most is with the issues that quagga mussels caused, over filtering the water, but also cleaning up longstanding pollution in Erie. The introduction of the gobi gave a predator for the quagga mussels, but also giving the native fish something to eat, after the selection for larger teeth.
